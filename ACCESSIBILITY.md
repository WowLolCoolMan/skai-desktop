# Accessibility

Skai Desktop aims to make its desktop workflows usable with a keyboard, screen
readers, and reduced-motion settings. The Electron renderer uses semantic
HTML, visible focus states, accessible names, and responsive layouts where
implemented. Support varies by operating system, runtime, and individual
feature; the project does not claim that every flow has completed live assistive
technology testing.

Known work and evidence are tracked in the [capability
matrix](docs/CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) and [To Do Next](docs/TO_DO_NEXT.md). If you
encounter an accessibility barrier, open an issue using the Accessibility
Barrier template and include:

- operating system and version;
- Skai Desktop version and the page or workflow;
- assistive technology, input method, and relevant settings;
- steps to reproduce and what happened versus what you expected.

Please omit private conversation contents and other sensitive information.
Screenshots or recordings are optional; do not include personal data without
permission from everyone shown.
