# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment
for everyone who participates in the Skai Desktop project, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience
level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and evidence; do not attack people.
- Accept good-faith correction and feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
attention without consent, and deliberate disruption of project spaces are not
acceptable. Unwelcome conduct may be addressed even if it occurs outside a
project space when it affects a participant's safety or ability to contribute.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality to
the extent practical, and take action appropriate to the circumstances.
Actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or
removal from project spaces. Maintainers will not publish private reports
without the reporter's consent unless required by law or necessary to address
an immediate safety concern.

## Reporting

Contact the repository owner through the private contact options on
https://github.com/WowLolCoolMan. Do not include private information in a public
issue. For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting instructions in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code review, and other
spaces operated for this project.
